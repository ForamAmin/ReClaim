About ReClaim: UniConnect

ReClaim: UniConnect is a platform dedicated to helping students reclaim and connect with their academic journeys. Our mission is to provide resources, support, and a community for students navigating the challenges of university life.

Our Features

  • Lost and Found: A system to help students report and recover lost items on campus.

How ?

Students can create accounts, report lost items, and browse found items. Our platform uses a user-friendly interface to make the process seamless and efficient.
