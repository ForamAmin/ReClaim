What makes us different
Built on three pillars.

Secure

Only verified campus students can access, report, or claim items. Every claim is gated behind ownership questions so the right person always gets their stuff back.

Fast

Instant reporting, real-time dashboard updates, and email notifications mean you'll hear about your item as soon as it's found — not days later.

Community

Built by students, for students. Every interaction is designed to encourage honesty, good faith, and genuine campus connection.

Origin

Made on campus, for campus.

ReClaim started because we kept seeing the same problem — lost wallets on notice boards, unclaimed bags at security, frustrated students. We knew there had to be a better way.

Values

Honesty. Speed. Trust.

We believe that most people are fundamentally good. ReClaim is designed to remove friction and give good people the tools to do the right thing, easily.
