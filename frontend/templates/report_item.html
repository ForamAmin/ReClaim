Tips for a Good Report
Take a clear, well-lit photo of the item before handing it in.
Describe unique details — scratches, stickers, initials — that only the owner would know.
Be specific about the location. "Near the left window in Library Room 2" beats "Library."
Submit as soon as possible — items reported quickly get returned twice as fast.
